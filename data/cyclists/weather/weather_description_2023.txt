date: Date of observation day in DD.MM.YYYY format (day first, month second, four digit year). 
airPressure_7h: Air pressure at 07h in hecto Pascal [hPa]. 
airPressure_14h: Air pressure at 14h (2 pm) in hecto Pascal [hPa]. 
airPressure_19h: Air pressure at 19h (7 pm) in hecto Pascal [hPa]. 
skyCover_7h: Sky cover (amount of opaque clouds covering the sky) at 07h on a scale from [1..10], where 10 is full coverage. 
skyCover_14h: Sky cover (amount of opaque clouds covering the sky) at 14h (2 pm) on a scale from [1..10], where 10 is full coverage. 
skyCover_19h: Sky cover (amount of opaque clouds covering the sky) at 19h (7 pm) on a scale from [1..10], where 10 is full coverage. 
temp_7h: Air temperature at 07h in degree Fahrenheit [°F]. 
temp_14h: Air temperature at 14h (2 pm) in degree Fahrenheit [°F]. 
temp_19h: Air temperature at 19h (7 pm) in degree Fahrenheit [°F]. 
hum_7h: Humidity at 07h in percent [%]. 
hum_14h: Humidity at 14h (2 pm) in percent [%]. 
hum_19h: Humidity at 19h (7 pm) in percent [%]. 
windDir_7h: Direction of wind at 07h in compass directions. 
windBeauf_7h: Strength of wind at 07h in Beaufort scale. 
windDir_14h: Direction of wind at 14h (2 pm) in compass directions. 
windBeauf_14h: Strength of wind at 14h (2 pm) in Beaufort scale. 
windDir_19h: Direction of wind at 19h (7 pm) in compass directions. 
windBeauf_19h: Strength of wind at 19h (7 pm) in Beaufort scale. 
precip: Daily precipitation in millimeters [mm]. "traces" indicates a precipitation < 0.1mm 
precipType: Precipitation type.
